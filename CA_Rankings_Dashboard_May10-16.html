How to Read These Cards
First to Latest Week
Compares a location's very first week on record to the most recent week. Shows the overall direction of travel regardless of week-to-week fluctuations. Best for seeing the big picture over time.
Week over Week
Shows each individual week's change step by step. Useful for spotting whether momentum is building, slowing, or reversing. A location may improve overall but show a dip in the middle week.
Consecutive Streak
Only shows locations that improved or declined every single week without exception. The strictest filter — a true streak with no off weeks. Becomes more meaningful as weeks accumulate. Empty early in the year is normal.
