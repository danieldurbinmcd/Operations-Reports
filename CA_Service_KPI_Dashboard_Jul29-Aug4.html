How to Read Your Score

1.00 means you meet the standard on balance. Above 1.00, you're ahead. Below, you're behind. Scores are capped at 0.50–1.50 so no single extreme metric can dominate the composite.

Effective DT Time (50% weight) — Timer OEPE + Parked% × Parked Time. Uses the magnetic plate timer (ungameable) where coverage is ≥ 80%. Falls back to bump basis with a red flag when timer is unavailable. Standard: 130s.

R2P Front Counter (15% weight) — Receipt-to-present time at front counter. Gated: below 30s scores 0.85 penalty (physically impossible — investigate receipt-timing practices). Standard: 45s.

KVS Time/Item (20% weight) — Production speed per item, not per order. A restaurant building complex orders is not penalized. Gated: below 50% healthy usage scores neutral 1.00 (data unreliable); below 12s/item scores 0.85 penalty (impossible number — investigate bump practices). Standard: 18s/item.

KVS Healthy Usage (15% weight) — Bump discipline integrity. Standard: 80%. Below 50% triggers the KVS trust gate above.

3PO Delivery Index (conditional, up to 15% weight) — Only for restaurants doing meaningful weekly delivery volume. Measures restaurant handoff time (60% weight) and order accuracy (40% weight). Driver drive time and courier ratings are not counted against you. Standard: 3.0 min handoff, 98% accuracy.
